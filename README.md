# Quaker Cereal Category Performance Analysis

### Retail Category Analytics | Power BI | Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project investigates a **50.8% sales decline for Quaker within the Cold Cereal category**, identifying the key factors contributing to the decline and opportunities to improve commercial performance.

The analysis examines brand performance, product-level trends, geographic differences, and selling presence to translate retail data into actionable business recommendations.

## Business Problem

Quaker experienced a substantial decline in sales performance relative to the broader cereal category.

The central business question was:

**What factors contributed to Quaker's sales decline, where were the losses concentrated, and which commercial opportunities should be prioritized for recovery?**

## Analytical Approach

The analysis focused on four areas:

1. **Category and brand performance:** Evaluating Quaker's performance relative to the overall category.
2. **Selling presence:** Investigating changes in selling store-weeks and their relationship with sales performance.
3. **Geographic performance:** Identifying markets contributing most to the decline.
4. **Product performance:** Examining individual products to identify major sources of sales losses.

## Key Findings

| Finding | Result |
|---|---|
| Quaker sales performance | −50.8% |
| Selling store-weeks | −30.1% |
| Geographic concentration | Ohio and Texas accounted for approximately 90% of the decline |
| Largest product-level loss | Cap'n Crunch Berries |
| Purchase behavior | Purchasing occasions declined while basket quantity remained relatively stable |

The findings suggest that reduced selling presence and fewer purchasing occasions were important contributors to the decline, rather than a substantial reduction in quantity purchased per occasion.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis identified opportunities to:

- Prioritize recovery efforts in the markets contributing most to lost sales.
- Investigate and address reductions in selling presence.
- Focus commercial attention on high-impact products.
- Monitor purchasing occasions and selling presence alongside sales performance.

These are analytical recommendations, not implemented interventions or verified recovery results.
